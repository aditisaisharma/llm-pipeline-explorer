Rotary Position Embedding (RoPE) Angle Phase Space

Tokens rotate in 2D coordinate pairs by angle $m \cdot \theta_i$. Distant tokens have larger relative phase angle.

Why RoPE is Revolutionary

Unlike absolute positional embeddings, RoPE preserves relative distance via complex rotation: $\langle R_{\Theta, m} q, R_{\Theta, n} k \rangle = g(q, k, m - n)$.

R_{\Theta, m}^d = \text{diag}(R_{\theta_1, m}, R_{\theta_2, m}, \dots)

RMSNorm (Modern Standard)

\text{RMSNorm}(x) = \frac{x}{\sqrt{\frac{1}{d}\sum_{i=1}^d x_i^2 + \epsilon}} \odot \gamma

Omits mean centering and bias $\beta$, saving ~7% memory bandwidth with identical performance.

LayerNorm (Classic Standard)

\text{LayerNorm}(x) = \frac{x - \mu}{\sqrt{\sigma^2 + \epsilon}} \odot \gamma + \beta

Centers around mean $\mu$ and tracks learnable bias $\beta$.
